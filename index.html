ShortsSystem Uploader

ShortsSystem Uploader is a private tool used by one creator to upload and schedule their own finished videos on their own YouTube channels (Mr.J - Gaming, MRJGTA, TechDailyBiz, Naak-aam and DopaharLofi).

It uses the official YouTube Data API to upload a video, set its title, description, tags, thumbnail and publish time, and post the creator's own comment on that video. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
