Privacy Policy

ShortsSystem Uploader. Last updated: 1 November 2026.

What the app does

ShortsSystem Uploader uploads and schedules videos on the YouTube channels of the person who runs it, using the YouTube API Services. It is used only by its owner, for the owner's own channels.

Data it uses

  • A Google sign-in token for each connected YouTube channel, created when the owner signs in and grants access.
  • The channel's name, read once to confirm which channel was connected.
  • The video files, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, publish times and comment text that the owner chooses to upload.

How the data is stored and shared

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a folder readable only by the owner's user account.
  • No data is sent anywhere except to Google/YouTube, to perform the uploads the owner asked for.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or analysed.
  • The app does not read or store data about any other YouTube user.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account > Third-party connections. Deleting the token files on the owner's computer also removes the stored sign-in.
